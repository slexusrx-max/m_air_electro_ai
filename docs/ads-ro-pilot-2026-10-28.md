# Romanian paid-search pilot

Status: prepared, NOT launched. Owner authorized up to EUR 50 TOTAL, Romania only, after readiness checks. No recurring budget, advertising spend or sales outcome is authorized by this document alone. No advertising money has been spent by this task.

## Campaign proposal

- Channel: Google Search; one narrowly scoped Romanian-language campaign. Exclude Display and search partners for this first test. No Shopping, Performance Max, remarketing, audience uploads or direct-to-merchant ads.
- Audience: people present in Romania, not merely interested in Romania. Do not expand geography automatically. Germany/other Europe require a separate budget/targeting decision; the Waterdrop email does not change the Romanian pilot.
- Intent: choosing a LiFePO4 leisure battery for an RV/caravan. Start with the existing battery category, whose documented Renogy models have active links. Do not spend this pilot on PECRON/BLUETTI or untracked feed rows.
- Final URL: https://mairelectroai.com/marketplace/batteries
- Measurement suffix on our own landing URL only: `utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=ro_battery_pilot_20261028&utm_content=rsa_a`. Preserve issued merchant tracking URLs unchanged. Do not put personal data in parameters.
- Draft name: `MAEAI | RO | Battery selection | Pilot 50 EUR`.
- Timing: seven days beginning only after every release gate below passes; no start date has been scheduled.
- Financial guard: maximum EUR 50 including applicable taxes/fees and exchange effects. Proposed media budget no more than EUR 40, reserving EUR 10 until actual billing charges/currency are confirmed. Use a campaign TOTAL budget and explicit end date; never mistake a daily budget for this cap. If the account cannot enforce the cap, do not launch. No automatic top-ups or extensions.
- Initial bid proposal: Maximize clicks with a CPC ceiling equivalent to EUR 0.25 if supported. This is a cost-control experiment, not a CPC/traffic forecast. No conversion-based bidding until genuine conversion measurement exists.

## Launch gates

1. Existing Impact contract or explicit brand response permits non-brand paid search to this independent catalogue, including trademark/direct-link exclusions. Romania eligibility alone is not PPC permission.
2. Verify affiliate clicks in the network with a documented reporting period/timezone; do not create bulk synthetic clicks or self-purchases. An eligible action is separate from a click and a locked/approved commission is separate from an action.
3. Read-only check of payment-profile readiness: identity/tax verification where applicable, approved bank/PayPal destination, withdrawal conditions and any holds. Owner enters financial information privately; never commit it or change payout details from this task.
4. Configure a legitimate existing analytics destination and verify consented receipt of supplier-click/calculator events. These are secondary events, not purchases or revenue. Missing data is UNKNOWN, not zero. Do not create a fake purchase conversion to optimize bidding.
5. Finish Google Ads account and billing setup, review the terms with the owner and resolve its ad-blocker notice. Current account chooser reports no Ads accounts. No terms or payment details were submitted in this task.
6. Verify the all-in financial cap, final landing URLs, mobile usability, disclosures and ad-policy eligibility. Keep the campaign paused until complete.

## Monitoring and decision rules

Review during the existing daily growth run, not a duplicate schedule. Separate Google impressions/clicks/spend, consented on-site events, Impact clicks, pending actions, locked/approved commission and paid withdrawals. Keep currency, date range, timezone and definition with each number. Clicks are not purchases and merchant order value is not affiliate income. Do not optimize to impressions alone or claim ROAS without attributed commission evidence.

Stop or keep paused for broken destinations, removed approvals, unverified paid-channel rights, missing measurement, spending-control failures or unsupported delivery claims. A seven-day/EUR 50 pilot may be inconclusive; never promise sales. Do not automatically increase the budget after a weak or successful test. Notify only a material failure, new approval/reply, verified release or meaningful measured result.
