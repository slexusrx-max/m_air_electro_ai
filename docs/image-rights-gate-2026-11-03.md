# Product image rights gate — 3 November 2026

M Air Electro AI temporarily withholds 29 product photographs whose exact official source was verified but whose publication permission or approved partner creative access was not confirmed:

- 6 BLUETTI records from `data/bluetti-reviewed-photos.json`
- 9 PECRON base records from `data/pecron-reviewed-photos.json`
- 14 PECRON expansion records from `data/pecron-reviewed-expansion.json`

The product pages, factual specifications, search, comparison and ordinary manufacturer links remain available. The UI uses its neutral “Product image awaiting verification” placeholder and never substitutes another model.

The source records remain in the repository for provenance. They do not establish publication rights. Site-owner approval does not replace permission from the rights holder.

A withheld image may be re-enabled only after one of these is documented:

1. explicit written permission covering this site and intended promotion; or
2. approved partner-program access to the specific creative with applicable usage terms.

Reactivation must preserve the exact model-to-image mapping and update automated tests and the photo-credits page.
