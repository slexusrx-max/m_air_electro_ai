How much battery do your essential appliances need?

Start with the devices you need and how many hours they must run. Wh describe energy; W describe the power your equipment must deliver. A refrigerator’s compressor startup matters separately from its average consumption.

Our guide walks through an illustrative calculation and its limits. Use measured consumption from your own appliances before choosing a station or battery.
